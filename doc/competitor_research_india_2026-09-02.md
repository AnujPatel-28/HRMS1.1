## factoHR

**1. Module taxonomy.** factoHR's own site lists these as separate product pages: Recruitment Software, HR Software (Core HR), Employee Onboarding, Attendance Management System, Payroll Software, Leave Management System, Performance Management Software, Learning Management System, Travel & Expense, Employee Self Service, Mobile App, Timesheet Software, and Integration — 13 primary product pages, plus a distinct "Retirement Benefits" family (Provident Fund, Superannuation Fund, Gratuity, GPF, Pension Management) marketed separately. ([factohr.com/hr-software](https://factohr.com/hr-software/))

**2. Modularity & pricing.** Pricing is tiered, not à-la-carte. `factohr.com/plans/` shows: **Free** (₹0, ≤20 employees, Employee Data/Letters/Payslips only), **Core** (₹4,999/mo for 50 employees + ₹69/employee/mo — adds Onboarding, Core HR, Attendance & Leave, Payroll, Dashboard, Helpdesk, Mobile/ESS, Multi-entity), **Premium** (₹5,999/mo, adds Travel/Expense/Reimbursement, Geo-Fence), **Ultimate** (₹6,999/mo, adds Performance, Survey & Acknowledgment). Each tier is strictly additive over the last, so there is no "attendance-only, no payroll" SKU — Core already bundles attendance *and* payroll together. A handful of true add-ons exist outside the tiers: Selfie Punch ($0.5/mo), Face Recognition ($1/mo), Timesheet ($0.5/mo), Fia AI chatbot ($0.5/mo), Recruitment ($75/mo per recruiter). ([factohr.com/plans](https://factohr.com/plans/)) A third-party listing quotes factoHR's own marketing as "You can select the modules according to your need," but the pricing page itself shows no standalone attendance-without-payroll purchase path. ([softwaresuggest.com/factohr](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/factohr))

**3. Employee lifecycle.** factoHR explicitly frames "every stage of the employee lifecycle" as: **Preboarding → Onboarding → Employee Management → Transfers/Promotions → Employee Engagement → Offboarding**, presented as a narrative arc across the product pages rather than a single unified UI object. ([factohr.com/hr-software](https://factohr.com/hr-software/))

**4. Cross-module seams.** The named seam artifact is **"Submit Attendance"**: a screen that "prepares data like number of Full days, Half days, Week-offs, Leaves, Holidays, Late and Early count, LOP etcetera for the selected attendance period. Once saved, this is used for salary calculation on prorate basis." ([help.factohr.com/knowledgebase/submitattendance](https://help.factohr.com/knowledgebase/submitattendance/), [help.factohr.com/knowledgebase/payroll](https://help.factohr.com/knowledgebase/payroll/)) Marketing copy adds: if biometric/mobile attendance isn't integrated, "the time-keeper can upload monthly total Present or LOP/Absenteeism days for calculating salary" — i.e., a manual fallback path for customers not using factoHR's own attendance capture. LOP is glossary-defined as salary deduction "when the available leave balance is exhausted." ([factohr.com/hr-glossary/loss-of-pay](https://factohr.com/hr-glossary/loss-of-pay/)) For export to external systems, factoHR documents "Auto Payroll JV" with export to Excel/XML/CSV — evidence of an export path but no public REST API docs found ("Integration" is listed as a product but its API surface is not detailed on public pages — not found).

**5. Configurability.** Payroll: a **"Flexible Rule Engine"** ("formula-driven rule engine") lets orgs "create unlimited earning or deduction components" with calculation types (formula-based / % of CTC / fixed) and "Multiple Payroll Groups" by cycle/type/calendar/location, plus 15+ payslip formats. ([factohr.com/payroll-software](https://factohr.com/payroll-software/)) Attendance: a "flexible rule engine and advanced policy configuration" covering work-hour calculation, break durations, overtime/comp-off per employee category, late/early/half-day deduction rules. ([factohr.com/attendance-management-system](https://factohr.com/attendance-management-system/)) No dedicated named "workflow engine" product was found for approvals; Performance module has "Multi-Level Reviews" up to four levels ([factohr.com/performance-management-software](https://factohr.com/performance-management-software/)).

**Distinctive/other:** Multi-shift support includes "auto-shift, time-based, and flexible shifts" with roster planning by day/week/biweek/month and employee-initiated shift-change requests. Biometric integration: ESSL, ZKTeco, Time Office, Real Time, Secureye, Matrix, BioMax, connected via "database, IP, or SDK." Performance module includes OKRs, 360° feedback (with anonymity option), Bell Curve normalization, Perception Gap Analysis, and **"Performance Linked-Payroll"** (incentive calc tied to review scores) — a named cross-module link worth noting. ([factohr.com/attendance-management-system](https://factohr.com/attendance-management-system/), [factohr.com/performance-management-software](https://factohr.com/performance-management-software/))

## HROne

**1. Module taxonomy.** The vendor's marketing site (`go.hrone.cloud`) names five headline modules — **Workforce, Payroll, Attendance, Performance, Recruitment** — plus a "More Modules" row: Helpdesk, Expense, Learning, Engagement, Assets. ("HROne offers eight powerful modules, deeply integrated.") The pricing page uses slightly different naming for the same underlying capability: **"Time Office"** rather than "Attendance." ([go.hrone.cloud](https://go.hrone.cloud/), [hrone.cloud/pricing](https://hrone.cloud/pricing/))

**2. Modularity & pricing.** Also tiered, not à-la-carte, per `hrone.cloud/pricing/`: **Basic** ₹4,950/mo for 50 users (+₹99/user) — Core HR, Time Office, Payroll, Mobile App, Reports; **Professional** ₹6,500/mo (+₹130/user) — Basic plus Workforce (confirmation/transfer/separation); **Enterprise** — custom quote, adds Recruitment, Performance, Engagement, Expense, Asset, Helpdesk. The page states plans are "charged per user basis," and the comparison table gates whole feature groups behind tier upgrade rather than per-module purchase — no attendance-only SKU. Add-ons available on any tier (separately quoted): Payroll Outsourcing, WhatsApp Bot, Teams Bot, Work Plan, Business Intelligence, Workforce Planning. A "Startup Launchpad" offers reduced pricing under 50 employees. ([hrone.cloud/pricing](https://hrone.cloud/pricing/)) Note: a third-party aggregator's claim of a flat ₹99/user/month "all modules included" price could not be corroborated against the vendor's own tiered page and is not used here — treat as unverified.

**3. Employee lifecycle.** HROne's **Workforce module is explicitly pitched as the lifecycle owner**: "Manage the entire employee lifecycle from hire to retire," and the site's tagline repeats "hire to retire" covering onboarding through offboarding. Unlike factoHR's narrative framing, HROne ties lifecycle language to one specific named module (Workforce) rather than treating it as a cross-cutting concept spanning all modules. ([go.hrone.cloud](https://go.hrone.cloud/))

**4. Cross-module seams.** Payroll page states: "Attendance and leave data flow in automatically from Time Office – Loss of Pay is computed, not typed," and separately markets that payroll and attendance being "built into the same system" removes the need for external integration. ([hrone.cloud/hr-software/payroll](https://hrone.cloud/hr-software/payroll/)) Rather than a named rule engine, HROne emphasizes a **pre-run anomaly detector**: a "smart algorithm" that "compares the run against last month and produces an exception list" for HR to review before disbursal. No public documentation of an export/API path for customers who use HROne attendance but an external payroll (or vice versa) was found — not found.

**5. Configurability.** Attendance: shift configuration covers "fixed shifts (morning, afternoon, night), rotating shifts, split shifts, flexible work hours, and weekend shifts," with "shift allowances configured at the shift level and applied automatically in payroll" and "shift rotation policies automate roster creation." Leave: configurable types (Earned/Casual/Sick/Maternity/Paternity/Comp-Off/custom) with carry-forward rules, accrual caps, and "leave credit schedulers." Overtime rate is configurable (1.5x/2x/fixed). ([hrone.cloud/hr-software/attendance](https://hrone.cloud/hr-software/attendance/)) Payroll: "automated CTC calculator," configurable pay groups "as per organizational structure," "multiple and customizable pay components" — but no named formula-builder product was found (vendor language is looser than factoHR's "Rule Engine"). No dedicated named "workflow engine" for approvals found; approval routing is referenced only implicitly (leave approvals, offer approvals).

**Distinctive/other:** Biometric integration explicitly names **ESSL, ZKTeco, Suprema, BIOMAX**. Five check-in methods: biometric, mobile app, GPS geo-tagging, geofencing, WhatsApp, web portal — WhatsApp-based punching is a notably distinctive channel not seen on factoHR's pages. Compliance automation includes VPF/pension split on PF, ESI monthly files, TDS under both tax regimes with Form 24Q, PT by state, LWF, with a "built-in compliance checklist" tracking due dates. Performance module: goal setting, OKRs, appraisal cycles (quarterly/bi-annual/monthly/annual), continuous feedback loop — 360° review terminology was not explicitly found on the fetched page (implied via "peer input," not confirmed as a named feature).

## Notable ideas worth stealing

- factoHR's **"Submit Attendance" as an explicit, named, savable artifact** (not just a background sync) makes the attendance→payroll handoff auditable and gives HR a review checkpoint before payroll runs off it.
- factoHR documents a **manual-fallback path** (time-keeper uploads Present/LOP counts) for customers who don't use its own attendance capture — a concrete pattern for our "modules can work independently" requirement.
- HROne's **pre-payroll exception list** ("compares the run against last month," flags anomalies) is a cheap, high-value safety net worth building into any payroll run screen regardless of our rule-engine design.
- HROne's **WhatsApp-based punch-in** is a distinctive, low-friction attendance channel neither obvious nor commonly discussed — worth evaluating for field/blue-collar workforces.
- factoHR's **"Performance Linked-Payroll"** — an explicit named bridge from performance scores to incentive calculation — is a cross-module seam most vendors leave implicit; naming it as a product concept could clarify our own payroll-input contract.
- Both vendors gate modules by **tier, not by genuine independent purchase** — neither offers "attendance only, no payroll" as a real SKU despite marketing "modularity." This is a gap/differentiator we could exploit if our architecture already supports true per-module independence.
- factoHR's **JV export to Excel/XML/CSV** is a minimal but real interoperability seam for customers who want payroll data in an external GL/accounting system — a low-cost feature to match.
- HROne folding **Recruitment, Onboarding, and offboarding-adjacent actions (confirmation/transfer/separation) into one "Workforce" module** rather than scattering them suggests a UX pattern: one lifecycle-status timeline per employee, not siloed forms per HR event.
- Both vendors publish **named biometric device compatibility lists** (ESSL/ZKTeco/Suprema/BioMax/Matrix/Secureye) on their marketing pages as a trust signal — worth doing the same once our device ingest seam (B8) is stable.
